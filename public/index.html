Why CivicTrack

Built to fix exactly what breaks in traditional civic complaint systems.

Traditional Reporting

  • ✕ Duplicate reports pile up when multiple citizens report the same issue independently
  • ✕ Complaints are manually categorized or left uncategorized entirely
  • ✕ No centralized system to track a complaint from submission to resolution
  • ✕ Citizens have zero visibility into whether their complaint was even received
  • ✕ Disorganized manual processes delay even straightforward fixes

With CivicTrack

  • ✓ Geo-proximity + text similarity automatically flags likely duplicates
  • ✓ A trained classifier auto-categorizes every report from its description
  • ✓ One live dashboard tracks every complaint through its full lifecycle
  • ✓ Citizens see their own report's status update in real time
  • ✓ Digital submission and routing take seconds, not days
